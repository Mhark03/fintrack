Research Proposal Documentation

EMC-FINTRACK: A WEB-BASED FINANCIAL TRANSPARENCY SYSTEM FOR STUDENT ORGANIZATIONS ON EMC CAMPUS

Eastern Mindoro College, Inc. • Faculty of Information System

Abstract & Background

Traditional manual financial reporting methods frequently result in unclear, delayed, or inaccessible financial summaries, creating student skepticism and reduced organizational participation. EMC-FinTrack provides a reliable, standardized, and accessible digital financial reporting platform featuring digital receipt uploading, ledgers, real-time budget dashboards, and role-based access control.

Theoretical & Conceptual Framework

  • DeLone & McLean IS Success Model: Assesses system quality, information quality, service quality, and user satisfaction.
  • Agency & Accountability Theory: Eliminates information asymmetry between student principals and student leader agents.
  • IPO Model: Input (User profiles & requirements) $\rightarrow$ Process (System design, coding & testing) $\rightarrow$ Output (Deployed EMC-FinTrack Portal).
