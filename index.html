Research Documentation

EMC-FINTRACK: A WEB-BASED FINANCIAL TRANSPARENCY SYSTEM FOR STUDENT ORGANIZATIONS ON EMC CAMPUS

Eastern Mindoro College, Inc. • College of Information System

Lead Researcher & Developer:

Mark Anthony Acuzar

Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS)

Abstract & Objectives

Traditional manual financial reporting frequently results in unclear, delayed, or inaccessible summaries. EMC-FinTrack provides a standardized, reliable, and accessible web-based portal featuring digital receipt uploading, expense ledgers, real-time budget dashboards, and role-based access control for students, leaders, and advisers.

Theoretical Framework

  • DeLone & McLean IS Success Model: Measures system quality, information quality, and user satisfaction.
  • Agency & Accountability Theory: Reduces information asymmetry between student members (principals) and leaders (agents).
